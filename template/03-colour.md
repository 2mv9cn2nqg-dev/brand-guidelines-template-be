## Proportions

State how much space each colour takes on average.

> Example: 60 percent background and white, 30 percent primary colour, 10 percent accent.

## Legibility

Check every combination of text and background with a contrast checker. The accessibility guideline (WCAG 2.1, level AA) asks for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text.

A typeface used for a business needs a licence that allows that. Check in particular whether the licence covers website, print and apps.

The fallback is needed because clients and suppliers often do not have your typeface installed. Pick one with a similar width to your main typeface so text does not reflow.

## Language support

Check that the typeface has every character you need for Dutch and French: é, è, ê, ë, ï, ô, ç, œ, the IJ ligature and the euro sign. Also test quotation marks and the hyphen.

## Rules

- Use capitals sparingly, for example only for short labels.
- Do not set long passages in italic or bold.
- Write headings as normal sentences: capital only on the first letter and on proper names.

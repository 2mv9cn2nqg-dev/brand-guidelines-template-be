# 2. Logo

This chapter records which versions of your logo exist, how large they must be at minimum, and what you never do with them.

| Compact version | [file name] | SVG, PDF | Small spaces, for example a profile picture |
| Symbol | [file name] | SVG, PNG | Favicon, app icon, social media |

For the web, supply SVG or PNG with a transparent background. For print, supply PDF or SVG as vector artwork. A logo that only exists as a JPG has to be redrawn before it can be printed large.

## Clear space

Keep an empty zone around the logo. Measure it in the height of a recognisable part of the logo, such as the letter x or the symbol. Place no text, borders or image edges inside that zone.

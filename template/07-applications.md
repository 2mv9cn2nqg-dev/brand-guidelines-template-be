## Legal details checklist

This list is a starting point, not legal advice. Which details apply depends on your legal form, your sector and your activity. Check with your business counter (ondernemingsloket), your accountant or the FOD Economie.

On invoices, typically:

- Date of the invoice and a unique number
- Your name or company name, address and legal form
- Enterprise number and VAT number
- The customer's name and address, and their VAT number when relevant
- A description of the goods or services, the quantity and the price
- The VAT rate and the VAT amount, or the legal reason why no VAT is charged
- Payment terms and bank details

On websites and emails, typically:

- Company name and legal form
- Geographical address
- Email address and a way to contact you quickly
- Enterprise number and VAT number
- Where you are registered, if you are a company
- Details of any regulated profession and the supervising body
- A privacy statement that explains how you handle personal data
- A cookie policy, if your site uses cookies that need consent

On quotes and general terms:

- Whether your general terms apply, and where the customer can read them
- Validity period of the quote
- Price including or excluding VAT, stated clearly

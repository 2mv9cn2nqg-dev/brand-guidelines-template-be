# Brand guidelines: Bakkerij De Gouden Korst

This is a fictional example. The business, its details and the numbers are invented to show what a filled-in template looks like.

## 1. Brand

| Field | Fill in |
| --- | --- |
| Company name | Bakkerij De Gouden Korst |
| Legal form | Sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) |
| Enterprise number | BE 0123.456.789 (fictional) |
| Sector | Bakery |
| Region | Mechelen and surroundings |
| Founded | 2019 |

What we do: We bake traditional bread and pastries for families and small cafés around Mechelen.

Who it is for: People who would rather buy bread from a baker they know, and who accept paying a bit more for it.

Why we exist: Good bread takes time. We would rather bake fewer loaves properly than many loaves quickly.

Three words: warm, honest, local. Not: luxurious, anonymous, rushed.

Promise: Every loaf in the shop was baked that morning.

## 2. Logo

| Variant | File name | Format | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary, colour | korst-logo-kleur | SVG, PDF | Default |
| Primary, white | korst-logo-wit | SVG, PDF | On dark backgrounds |
| Primary, black | korst-logo-zwart | SVG, PDF | Stamps, one-colour print |

Clear space: the height of the letter K on all sides.
Minimum width: 25 mm in print, 120 px on screen. Symbol: 10 mm and 24 px.
Placement: top left on documents, centred on bags and boxes.

Not allowed: stretching, extra effects, placing the logo on a busy photo, using other colours.

## 5. Imagery

We photograph in the bakery in daylight, from eye level, close to the hands and the product. No posed stock photos. Photos of customers are only published with written permission, kept in the shop folder.

Icons: simple line icons, 2 px, round corners, in crust brown.

## 6. Tone

Form of address: "je" in Dutch with private customers, "u" with businesses. "Vous" in French.

Voice: friendly and direct. Short sentences. We say what is in the oven today and what is sold out.

| Situation | This is how we sound | Not like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greeting | Hallo Sofie, je bestelling staat klaar. | Geachte klant, uw order is gereed. |
| Mistake | Sorry, we hebben je brood vergeten in te pakken. Het komt morgen gratis mee. | Wij betreuren het ongemak. |

Language rules: dates as 29 oktober 2026, amounts as € 4,50, name always "Bakkerij De Gouden Korst".

## 7. Applications

Letterhead: A4, logo top left at 25 mm, footer with address, enterprise number and VAT number.
Invoice: numbering 2026-001, payment within 14 days.
Website: favicon is the symbol, social share image 1200 x 630 px, cookie banner with only essential cookies.
Social media: one Instagram account, same name and profile image as the website.

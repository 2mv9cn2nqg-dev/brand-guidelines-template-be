# Brand guidelines template for small Belgian businesses

A set of fill-in Markdown files for documenting the visual identity and tone of a sole proprietorship, partnership or small company. You fill in the fields, delete the example text, and end up with a document you can hand to a printer, a web developer or a new hire.

The template is written with Belgium in mind: Dutch and French side by side, the legal details that belong on invoices and websites, and the reality of small business owners who have no branding agency. The text is in English so it is easy to find and reuse. A Dutch version lives in the `nl/` folder.

## Who it is for

- Self-employed people and small companies putting their brand on paper for the first time
- Designers who want a fixed structure to use with clients
- Anyone who wants to hand over an existing visual identity without it living in one person's head

## How to use it

1. Fork or copy this repository.
2. Open the files in `template/` in order.
3. Replace every piece of text in `[brackets]` with your own.
4. Delete the lines that start with `> Example:` once you are done.
5. Export to PDF with a Markdown editor or with Pandoc if you want a printable document.

## Contents

| File | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| `template/01-brand.md` | Who you are, what you promise, who you serve |
| `template/02-logo.md` | Logo versions, clear space, minimum size, misuse |
| `template/03-colour.md` | Colour codes for screen and print |
| `template/04-typography.md` | Typefaces, sizes, line spacing |
| `template/05-imagery.md` | Photography, icons, illustration |
| `template/06-tone.md` | Writing style in Dutch and French |
| `template/07-applications.md` | Letterhead, invoice, website, social media, legal details |

A fully filled-in fictional example is in `example/bakery-de-gouden-korst.md`.

## What this is not

It is not a brand strategy. The template records decisions you have already made. If you have no name, positioning or logo yet, start there and come back.

The legal details in chapter 7 are a checklist, not legal advice. Check them against your own legal form with your business counter (ondernemingsloket) or your accountant.
